#### What makes me different
Data engineering and AI engineering started as two separate careers; the
boundary between them is where the most interesting work happens now.
MIT Technology Review found data engineers' time on AI projects nearly
doubled between 2023 and 2025 (19% to 37%), and hybrid titles like
"AI Data Engineer" are showing up in job postings. I work at that seam:
ELT pipelines that feed LLM-scored products, agentic workflows that query
production databases through MCP servers, open-source systems maintained
by their own LLMs. Everything above is live, tested, deployed and
monitored - not a demo deck.
